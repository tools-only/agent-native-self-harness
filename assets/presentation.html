14 · Takeaways
Q3 2021 IN PERSPECTIVE

Three things to remember

01 Strong reported earnings—with nonrecurring help

$11.7B net income included $2.1B of net reserve releases and a $566M tax benefit. Excluding significant items: $9.6B net income, $3.03 EPS.

02 Different businesses, different momentum

The economy continued to grow despite dampening from the Delta variant and supply-chain disruptions. Investment Banking, card spend and AWM assets were strong; CIB Fixed Income and CCB loan balances were weaker.

03 A well-capitalized firm returned capital

CET1 ratios were 12.9% Standardized and 13.6% Advanced; the firm paid $3.0B in dividends and made $5.0B of common stock net repurchases.

Glossary: EPS = earnings per share · CET1 = Common Equity Tier 1 · YoY = year over year · QoQ = quarter over quarter. ROE and ROTCE are equity-based return measures; managed figures are non-GAAP where identified.

Source: Earnings release, sections cited throughout (pp. 1–4). Figures and comparisons retain source labels.15 / 15
